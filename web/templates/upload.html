How it works

1️⃣ Upload a PDF or CSV bank/credit card statement

2️⃣ The local LLM extracts transactions in the background; the file is deleted once its text is read

3️⃣ Every row is checked: valid dates, non-zero amounts, text that really appears in the statement, and balances that add up

4️⃣ You review, fix or drop rows, then commit. Nothing is saved before that

5️⃣ Rows already in the ledger are skipped, and categorization rules are applied
